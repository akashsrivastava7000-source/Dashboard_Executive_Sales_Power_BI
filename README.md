📊 **Power BI Dashboard Project – Executive Sales Analysis**

I'm excited to share my latest **Power BI Dashboard – Executive Sales Analysis**, created to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The main objective of this project was to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, and product trends through an interactive dashboard.

**🔍 Key Insights & Features:**

✅ Total Revenue, Gross Profit, and Gross Margin  
✅ Total Quantity Sold and Total Orders  
✅ Monthly Revenue and Profit Analysis  
✅ Top 5 and Bottom 5 Countries by Revenue  
✅ Sales Channel Revenue Comparison  
✅ Product Category-wise Revenue Analysis  
✅ Interactive Slicers for Month, Quarter, Product Category, and Country  

**🛠️ Tools & Skills Used:**
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis
- DAX
- Data Modeling

This project helped me improve my practical understanding of data visualization and presenting business insights in a simple and effective way.
